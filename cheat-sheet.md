# Cheat sheet

Open Claude → **Code** → choose this folder. Put your clips in `footage/`. Finished videos appear in `out/`.

## First time only
"Set me up: check what this folder needs and install it, one step at a time. Explain each step in simple words and ask me before you install anything."

## Pick your video type
| Type | What you have | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Talking head | One short clip of you talking to the camera | 1 clean reel |
| 2 · Long recording | A workshop, podcast, live or long talk | 10+ reels |
| 3 · B-roll montage | Many short clips, nobody talking, plus a song | 1 reel cut to music |
| 4 · Singing over a song | A clip of you singing (no music) plus a song | 1 reel with your voice on the song |

## Type 1 · Talking head (1 clip → 1 clean reel)
1. "Cut the long pauses and the 'um's, clean up my voice, and add simple captions."
2. "Give me 3 hooks for this video: an opening line or a title for the screen. Don't edit yet."
3. "Use hook 2 as a title for the first 3 seconds. Then export."

## Type 2 · Long recording (1 long video → 10+ reels)
1. "This is a long recording. Find at least 10 parts that could each be their own reel: 30 to 60 seconds, one idea each. Make a 10-second sample video of each, numbered, with its time and a one-line summary."
2. "I like 2, 5 and 8. Give me 3 hooks for each: an opening line or a title for the screen."
3. "Make reel 2 with hook 1, reel 5 with hook 3 and reel 8 with hook 2. Cut the pauses and 'um's, clean the voice, add captions."

## Type 3 · B-roll montage (many clips + a song → 1 reel)
1. Idea: "Give me 5 ideas for this reel that aren't just 'a nice walk': a story, a feeling, a contrast or a question."
   Or: "My idea: [one line]. Which shots fit it, and what's missing?"
2. "Make a stills sheet of all my clips, with numbers."
3. "Here's my song. Use 30 seconds starting at 0:45 and find the beat."
4. "Make a 30-second vertical reel of idea 1, cut on the beat. Put a number on every shot."
5. "Keep 04. Move 05 after 02. Replace 06 with 21 from the sheet."
6. "Show me 3 colour looks on one frame."
7. "Add a title at the top, remove the numbers, export."

## Type 4 · Singing over a song (your voice + a track → 1 reel)
Film yourself singing, humming or harmonizing with no music playing (headphones are fine). Put the clip and the song in `footage/`.
1. "Here is my singing clip and the song. Find where my voice fits the song best. Tell me both keys and how well it fits. Don't edit yet."
2. "The music is [indie pop / sound healing / …]. Suggest 4 titles like 'Opera singer meets ___'."
3. "Use title 2. Keep it one take, start just before my first note, end after my last one. Export with the music, and also a version with only my voice."
4. Polish: "Add micro-zooms in my breaths between phrases, gentle: 1.0 to about 1.15x, centred on my face, never under the title."

## Polish (any type, one at a time, after the basic edit)
Each one makes a new version. You can always say "go back to the last version".

**Movement**
- Micro-zoom: "Add micro-zooms: switch between my normal framing and a slightly closer one (about 1.25x) at the cuts, every 3 to 5 seconds. Keep my face centred. Never zoom in the middle of a sentence."
- Slow push: "Add a very slow push-in over the whole video, so slow you barely notice it."
- Punch-in: "Zoom in on my face when I say the most important sentence, and hold it until the next cut."
- Reframe: "This clip is horizontal. Make it vertical and keep my face in the frame all the time."

**Text on screen**
- Behind me: "Put one big keyword behind me (my body in front of the text) when I say it. Max 3 in the whole video, none in the first 4 seconds."
- Captions: "Show the captions 3 words at a time, and make the word I'm saying brighter than the others."
- Ending: "Add a short call to action at the end for 3 seconds: 'follow for part 2'."

**Picture**
- Blur: "My room is messy. Blur the background softly and keep me sharp."
- Colour: "Make the colours a bit warmer, but keep my skin natural."
- B-roll over voice: "When I say 'the forest', show clip 12 from the stills sheet over my voice for 2 seconds."
- Freeze: "Freeze the frame on my face for 1 second after I say 'wait'."

**Sound and rhythm**
- Wind: "There's wind and background noise. Clean it up, but keep my voice natural."
- Music: "Put the song quietly under my voice. Lower it when I talk, a bit louder in the pauses, fade it out at the end."
- Swap song: "Replace the music with the new song. Keep everything else exactly the same."
- Whoosh: "Add a soft whoosh when the keyword appears. Very quiet."
- Pace: "Make it a little faster without making it feel rushed. Keep the pause after the joke."
- Speed: "Speed the whole video up to 1.1x, without making my voice sound higher."

Blur and the word behind you need Claude to find you in the picture. They can take a few tries.

## Steering
- "Too rushed. Keep pauses up to 1 second."
- "You cut a word I need at 0:12. Put it back."
- "The captions cover my mouth. Move them lower."
- "Start the video on the line about ..."
- Stuck? Describe what you see, not how to fix it. If it fails twice, ask Claude to try another way.

## Music you can post
- Safest: export without music, add the song inside Instagram or TikTok.
- OK: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay (free); Epidemic Sound, Artlist (paid).
- Don't: download a Spotify or YouTube song into the file.
